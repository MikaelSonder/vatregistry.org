GST/QST in Québec is the combined federal and provincial transaction-tax function through which taxable supplies, imports and business purchases are classified, invoiced, reported and documented under Canada’s GST framework and Québec’s QST system. It is broader than return filing alone because businesses must determine whether registration is required, whether 5 percent GST and 9.975 percent QST apply, how input tax credits and input tax refunds apply and how transaction evidence must be maintained.

Operationally, GST/QST in Québec usually begins with business model analysis rather than with tax form preparation. A business commonly reviews whether it is making domestic sales, imports, exports, intra-EU acquisitions, intra-EU dispatches, B2B services, consumer-facing supplies, digital transactions or mixed supplies, and then aligns registration, invoicing, accounting logic and reporting obligations with the actual commercial flow.

Québec’s system combines the federal Excise Tax Act for GST, the Act respecting the Québec sales tax for QST, Revenu Québec guidance, Québec’s separate registration and reporting administration, and Canadian place-of-supply rules. Compliance is shaped by the 5 percent GST, 9.975 percent QST, taxable, zero-rated and exempt treatment, small-supplier status, input tax credits, input tax refunds, digital-platform rules, tax invoices and interprovincial transactions.

Cross-border relevance is substantial. Québec is a major Canadian consumer, services, technology, manufacturing and e-commerce jurisdiction with a distinct provincial sales-tax system. Québec GST/QST must be coordinated with Revenu Québec registration, CBSA import treatment, place-of-supply rules, zero-rated exports, non-resident digital-supplier rules, customer location and other Canadian provincial tax systems.

Definition

GST/QST in Québec is the federal and provincial indirect-tax function through which taxable supplies, imports and business purchases are assessed, charged, documented and reported under Canadian GST and Québec QST rules. It concerns transaction taxes rather than business profit and affects Québec commerce, interprovincial trade, imports, exports, invoices and transaction evidence.

The practical importance of GST/QST lies in its recurring operational nature. It runs through sales, purchases, place-of-supply rules, invoice issuance, input tax credits, input tax refunds, reporting periods, imports, exports and interprovincial transaction control.

FAQ

The FAQ section collects recurring threshold questions in concise handbook form.

Is GST/QST in Québec only relevant for Québec companies?No. Foreign companies may also need Québec GST/QST analysis where they trade into Québec or create Québec GST/QST consequences.
Is GST/QST the same as income tax?No. GST/QST is a transactional consumption-tax system, while federal and Québec income taxes concern income or profit under separate rules.
Does every invoice automatically require Québec GST/QST?No. The correct treatment depends on registration, supply classification, place of supply, zero-rating or exemption status, customer location and Québec’s specified-registration rules.
Is return filing enough on its own?No. Effective GST/QST compliance also depends on invoices, records, coding logic, customer status analysis and supporting documentation.
Can interprovincial and international trade make Québec GST/QST more complex?Yes. Goods deliveries, service location, customer address, imports, exports, non-resident digital supplies and multi-province activity can create additional registration, rate, ITC/ITR and reporting layers.
What are the GST and QST rates in Québec?GST is 5 percent and QST is 9.975 percent, calculated on the selling price excluding GST. The combined effective tax rate is 14.975 percent on most taxable Québec supplies.
Who administers GST and QST in Québec?Revenu Québec administers both the federal GST and the provincial QST for businesses operating in Québec. This distinguishes Québec from most other provinces, where CRA administers GST/HST directly.
When is GST/QST registration required?A business generally must register when worldwide taxable supplies of the business and associated persons exceed CAD 30,000 in one calendar quarter or in the preceding four calendar quarters. Registration is generally voluntary below this small-supplier threshold.
What is the specified QST registration system?The specified QST system applies to certain non-resident suppliers and digital-platform operators making taxable supplies to specified Québec consumers. Registration is generally required once relevant Québec supplies exceed CAD 30,000 over a 12-month period.
What are ITCs and ITRs?Input tax credits (ITCs) allow recovery of eligible GST paid or payable on business inputs. Input tax refunds (ITRs) are the corresponding Québec QST recovery mechanism, subject to registration, documentary and business-use conditions.
